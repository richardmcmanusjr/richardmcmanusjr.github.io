LEADING INNOVATION

Prof. Sayeef Salahuddin

TSMC Distinguished Professor, EECS

Prof. Salahuddin leads the Berkeley Advanced Devices Lab with a vision to transform electronics through fundamental physics. His research has pioneered new paradigms in ferroelectrics, spintronics, and energy-efficient computing.

Recognized for advancing the scientific foundations of next-generation devices, his work bridges atomistic simulation with real-world device engineering.
