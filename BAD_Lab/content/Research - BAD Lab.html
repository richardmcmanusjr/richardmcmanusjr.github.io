OUR WORK

Research

Pushing the boundaries of physics and device engineering

What We Do

Three pillars of innovation in device engineering

Advanced Materials & Phenomena

Discovery and engineering of novel material systems—from ferroelectrics with negative capacitance to 2D heterostructures—with precisely engineered quantum and electronic properties

Theory & Simulation

First-principles modeling and atomistic simulation to understand emerging physics, electron transport, and device physics at the nanoscale that drives innovation

Device Engineering & Characterization

Precision nanofabrication, experimental validation, and characterization of prototype devices demonstrating breakthrough performance and enabling next-generation nanoelectronics

Research Frontiers

Where physics meets device innovation

01

Negative Capacitance

Pioneering work on negative capacitance phenomena in ferroelectric and ferrimagnetic materials for ultra-low power electronics and enhanced device performance

02

Ferroelectric & Multiferroic Materials

Exploring ferroelectric materials for memory and logic applications with superior energy efficiency and unprecedented switching speeds

03

Electron Device Physics

Investigating fundamental physics of electron transport and quantum phenomena in nanostructured materials for next-generation nanoelectronics

04

2D Materials & Heterostructures

Engineering atomically-thin materials with unique electronic and optical properties. The platform for future nanoelectronics and low-dimensional physics

05

Spin Phenomena & Magnetics

Harnessing spin-orbit torques and magnetic phenomena for low-power computing devices with fundamentally new physics

06

Beyond-CMOS Logic & Memory

Developing alternative computing paradigms including neuromorphic, quantum, and flexible electronics for energy-efficient information processing
