OUR WORK

Research

Pushing the boundaries of physics and device engineering

What We Do

Three pillars of innovation in device engineering

Advanced Materials & Phenomena

Discovery and engineering of novel material systems — from ferroelectrics, negative capacitance, andheterostructures—with precisely engineered quantum and electronic properties

Theory & Simulation

First-principles modeling and atomistic simulation to understand emerging physics, electron transport, and device physics at the nanoscale that drives innovation

Device Engineering & Characterization

Precision nanofabrication, experimental validation, and characterization of prototype devices demonstrating breakthrough performance

Unconventional Computing

Exploring novel computing paradigms for high-performance, energy-efficient information processing
